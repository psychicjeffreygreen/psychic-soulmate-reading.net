About Isabelle Fontaine

Isabelle Fontaine has spent twelve years on the part of love that most people ask about first and understand least: the when. Clients arrive asking how long they will have to wait. She gently turns the question around and asks what waiting has been like, because in her experience the useful part of timing is not a date on a calendar but the state of the person doing the waiting.

She works with intuitive astrology and psychic sensing, using the language of seasons and cycles as a way of talking about readiness. A season of rest, a season of clearing out, a season of opening: these are images she uses to help you see where you are, not forecasts that something will happen by a certain month. She will never give you a year, a deadline or a countdown, and she will tell you plainly that no one can.

What she can do is help you notice rhythm. Many people discover in a session with Isabelle that they have been rushing into connections when they were tired, or closing the door the moment someone showed real interest. By mapping your recent chapters in love as a sequence of seasons, she helps you see which habits belong to which phase, and what a more patient or a braver next step might look like.

Her tone is calm and slightly dry, and she is not afraid of a pause. Clients describe leaving her sessions with permission to stop treating their love life as a race, and with one or two small, practical commitments instead: a standing plan to meet new people, a boundary around an old story, a date night with themselves.

In the Red Thread Loom, our AI tool, you can choose Isabelle’s voice for the written letter. The tool is automated and only borrows her calm, seasonal style. It is not a live session with Isabelle, and it never predicts dates.

Pattern and rhythm mapping

How Isabelle reads

  • She names your season before she says anything else.
  • She uses cycles as images, never as appointments.
  • She replaces ‘when’ with ‘what helps now’.
  • She finishes with one small, practical commitment.

Questions Isabelle likes to work with

  • Am I in a season of waiting, healing or readiness?
  • Why do I rush in, or shut down, just when something starts?
  • How do I stop measuring my life against other people’s timelines?
  • What would a patient but active next three months look like?
  • How do I know I am not just avoiding love?

How a session with Isabelle works

  1. You sketch your recent chapters

    Share a few sentences about your last relationship, the gap since and how it has felt.

  2. Isabelle names the season

    She reflects the season she hears and the habits that tend to travel with it.

  3. You choose one commitment

    Together you pick a small step that suits your pace, and let the rest rest.

Readings are for reflection and entertainment. They do not predict fixed outcomes and are not a substitute for medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice.

Isabelle: frequently asked questions

Will Isabelle tell me when I will meet someone?

No. She does not give dates, months or years. She talks about seasons of readiness and what tends to help in each, as a way of reflecting on your own pace.

Is astrology required for a reading?

No. Isabelle uses it as a language for cycles. If you prefer to skip it, she will work from your story and her intuitive impressions alone.

Does the Red Thread Loom give a timeline?

No. In Isabelle’s voice it offers a season-of-life reflection and scene ideas, never a date. It is an automated tool, not a live session.

What if I feel behind everyone else?

That feeling comes up often. Isabelle will help you separate real information from pressure, and choose steps that fit your life rather than a schedule.
